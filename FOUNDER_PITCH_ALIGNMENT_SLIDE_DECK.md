## 🎯 1. Executive Summary & Pre-Seed Value Thesis

* **Target Investment Ask:** ₹9,00,000 INR ($10,800 USD) Pre-Seed Angel Round (Structured as 3 Tickets of ₹3 Lakhs for 3% equity each).
* **Core Value Thesis:** 650M digital sports fans in India are underserved by cluttered, legacy scorecard apps (Cricbuzz, ESPN) and friction-heavy live streaming. **SPORT IQ** delivers the **15-Second Match Pulse**—AI-curated 35-word bulletins, instant scorecard micro-graphics, and dual cricket/football parity with zero ad fatigue.
* **Technology Engine & Moat:** Automated scraping (Twitter/X commentary + RSS + Web stats) + official API feeds ingested into a **Cloud Data Lake**. Dialmate AI's Intelligence Layer processes this lake into **Short-Form Content** (15s short video scripts, 35-word cards, WhatsApp infographics) AND **Long-Form Content** (deep tactical match breakdowns, post-game analytical dossiers, weekly digests).
* **Capital Efficiency:** Zero tech vendor overheads through Dialmate AI incubation, delivering **88.5% gross margins** and achieving operational cashflow breakeven by Month 4.

## 📽️ 3. 10-Slide Pitch Deck Script & Visual Architecture

### Slide 1: Title & Vision
* **Headline:** SPORT IQ — The 15-Second Match Pulse for Next-Gen Sports Fans.
* **Sub-headline:** News • Insights • Scores • Stories | Incubated by Dialmate AI.
* **Speaker (Rajrup):** *"Every sports fan in India knows the frustration: you want to check the match state between meetings, but you're hit with 5 banner ads, a video pre-roll, or slow-loading scorecards. SPORT IQ changes that in 15 seconds."*
* **Visual:** Crimson/carbon glassmorphic frame with 3D match pulse ticker, ₹9L pre-seed badge, and 88.5% margin indicator.

### Slide 2: The Structural Market Void
* **Headline:** India's 650M Sports Audience is Trapped in Legacy Clutter.
* **Key Points:**
  1. **Legacy Scorecard Clutter:** Cricbuzz & ESPN monetize through aggressive banner ads, destroying speed and mobile UX.
  2. **Broadcast OTT Friction:** Hotstar & SonyLIV require high bandwidth and 30-minute time commitment—unusable for quick 30-second match pulse checks.
  3. **Fragmented Meme Feeds:** Social accounts post unverified data with zero real-time match telemetry.
* **Speaker (Rajrup):** *"Fans don't want a 20-minute highlight show while at work. They want the tactical pivot, the live score, and the viral context in one tap."*

### Slide 3: The Solution — SPORT IQ Experience
* **Headline:** Ultra-Fast, AI-Synthesized Sports Intelligence.
* **3-Pillar Loop:**
  * **Beat 1 (Score):** Live pins for Cricket (IPL, T20) and Football (ISL, Premier League, Champions League).
  * **Beat 2 (Brief):** 35-word natural language micro-bulletins explaining *why* momentum shifted.
  * **Beat 3 (Viral Card):** Auto-generated 1-click shareable match graphics for WhatsApp & Instagram.
* **Speaker (Sayan):** *"Here is our live PWA in action. Sub-800ms load time, offline caching, and dual-sport parity where cricket and football share equal front-page real estate."*

### Slide 4: Data Pipelines, Data Lake & AI Intelligence Engine
* **Headline:** End-to-End Automated Data Engine: Scrapers + APIs → Data Lake → Dialmate AI.
* **4-Step Architecture:**
  1. **Hybrid Ingestion:** Scrapers (Twitter/X commentary + RSS + Web stats) + Official Cricket & Football APIs.
  2. **Cloud Data Lake:** Serverless raw data repository storing play-by-play telemetry, audio/video transcripts, and fan sentiment.
  3. **Dialmate AI Intelligence Layer:** LLM & NLP context synthesizer rendering match insights in <2.5 seconds.
  4. **Dual Output Generation:**
     * **Shorts:** 15s short video scripts (Remotion), 35-word micro-cards, viral WhatsApp match graphics.
     * **Long-Form:** Deep tactical match breakdowns, post-game analytical dossiers, weekly newsletter digests.
* **Speaker (Sayan):** *"Our proprietary data lake and AI intelligence layer automatically converts millions of raw match commentary data points into both snackable 15-second shorts and comprehensive tactical long-form reports. Zero vendor burn, 100% automated."*

### Slide 5: Hyper-Lean Creator Syndication Engine
* **Headline:** Bypassing Ad Middlemen via 50+ Regional Micro-Creators.
* **Strategy:**
  * Contracting 50+ tier-2/tier-3 cricket & football micro-influencers (10k–70k followers) at ₹3,000–₹5,000/month retainers.
  * Moderated WhatsApp VIP groups & Telegram channels where daily match score cards are seeded for viral 1-click forwarding.
  * Target Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) capped strictly at **< ₹4.00/user**.
* **Speaker (Rajrup):** *"We don't burn money on broad Meta ads. We sponsor regional creators who already have fan trust, delivering an immediate 4.2% install conversion rate."*

### Slide 6: ₹9,00,000 Seed Capital Allocation
* **Headline:** Zero-Waste Capital Deployment Across an 8-Month Runway.
* **Budget Breakdown:**
  * **Micro-Creator & Growth Syndication (44%):** ₹4,00,000 (50+ creators + WhatsApp VIP + paid boosts).
  * **Data Lake & AI Pipeline Tech (23%):** ₹2,10,000 (Scrapers, API feeds, Supabase/AWS, LLM compute).
  * **Short Video & Asset Production (13%):** ₹1,20,000 (Remotion templates, motion packs, regional translators).
  * **Working Capital & Tournament Scaling (13%):** ₹1,20,000 (IPL/World Cup reserve buffer).
  * **Legal, Entity Incorporation & App Stores (7%):** ₹50,000 (Pvt Ltd, TM, Apple/Google developer licenses).
* **Speaker (Sayan):** *"Every rupee is milestone-gated across 3 disbursement tranches. Funds are released only upon hitting audited performance targets."*

### Slide 7: 6-Month Execution Roadmap & Breakeven Pathway
* **Headline:** Cashflow Breakeven by Month 4 & ₹5.85L MRR by Month 6.
* **Timeline:**
  * **Month 1:** MVP launch, Data Lake + Scrapers live, 35 creator pre-commitments.
  * **Month 2:** Closed Beta (8.5k MAU), WhatsApp VIP network (15k members), Shorts video script generator.
  * **Month 3:** Public launch (25k MAU), first B2B API syndication client (₹65k revenue).
  * **Month 4 (Cashflow Breakeven):** 50k MAU, ₹1,65,000 revenue vs ₹1,15,000 OpEx (net cashflow positive).
  * **Month 5:** 85k MAU, Long-Form Tactical Analytics Dossier Engine live, ₹3,10,000 revenue.
  * **Month 6 (Institutional Seed Pitch):** 130k MAU, ₹5,85,000 MRR, initiating Series Seed round (₹2.5 Cr @ ₹15 Cr valuation).
* **Speaker (Rajrup):** *"By Month 4, the company is self-funding. 100% of positive cashflow is recycled into expanding creator retainers and AI model capabilities."*

### Slide 8: 4-Pillar Monetization & Unit Economics
* **Headline:** Robust Revenue Architecture Delivering 88.5% Gross Margins.
* **Monetization Pillars:**
  1. **B2B API Syndication (35%):** Licensing bulletin feeds & short video templates to sports blogs & betting portals.
  2. **Creator Co-Sponsorships (35%):** Native brand badges on viral match cards & short videos.
  3. **SPORT IQ Pro Subscriptions (18%):** Premium B2C ad-free 15s pulse, fantasy prediction signals @ ₹99/mo.
  4. **Sports Affiliate Partnerships (12%):** Contextual merchandise (FanCode) & fantasy contest referrals.
* **Unit Economics:** Blended CAC: ₹3.80 | 6-Month LTV: ₹18.50 | LTV/CAC Ratio: 4.86x | Gross Margin: 88.5%.
* **Speaker (Sayan):** *"Our multi-stream monetization ensures we aren't reliant on ad CPMs alone. B2B API syndication and Pro subscriptions provide predictable recurring cashflow."*
