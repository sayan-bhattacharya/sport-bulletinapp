## 📽️ 3. 10-Slide Pitch Deck Script & Visual Architecture

### Slide 1: Title & Vision
* **Headline:** SPORT IQ — The 15-Second Match Pulse for Next-Gen Sports Fans.
* **Sub-headline:** News • Insights • Scores • Stories | Incubated by Dialmate AI.
* **Speaker (Rajrup):** *"Every sports fan in India knows the frustration: you want to check the match state between meetings, but you're hit with 5 banner ads, a video pre-roll, or slow-loading scorecards. SPORT IQ changes that in 15 seconds."*
* **Visual:** Sleek crimson/carbon frame with the 3D globe badge and live match ticker.

### Slide 2: The Structural Market Void
* **Headline:** India's 650M Sports Audience is Trapped in Clutter.
* **Key Points:**
  1. **Legacy Scorecard Clutter:** Cricbuzz & ESPN monetize through aggressive programmatic banner ads, destroying speed and mobile UX.
  2. **Broadcast Friction:** OTT streaming giants (JioHotstar, SonyLIV) require heavy bandwidth and focus—unusable for quick 30-second match pulse checks.
  3. **Fragmented Meme Feeds:** Social accounts post irregularly with unverified data and zero real-time telemetry.
* **Speaker (Rajrup):** *"Fans don't want a 20-minute highlight show while at work. They want the tactical pivot, the live score, and the viral context in one tap."*

### Slide 3: The Solution — SPORT IQ Experience
* **Headline:** Ultra-Fast, AI-Synthesized Sports Intelligence.
* **3-Pillar Loop:**
  * **Beat 1 (Score):** Live pins for Cricket (IPL, Tests, T20) and Football (ISL, Premier League, Champions League).
  * **Beat 2 (Brief):** 35-word natural language micro-bulletins explaining *why* momentum shifted.
  * **Beat 3 (Viral Card):** Auto-generated 1-click shareable match graphics for WhatsApp & Instagram.
* **Speaker (Sayan):** *"Here is our live PWA in action. Sub-800ms load time, offline-ready caching, and dual-sport parity where cricket and football share equal front-page real estate."*

### Slide 4: Technology Moat & Dialmate AI Backing
* **Headline:** Zero Tech Vendor Dependency = Asymmetric Margin Advantage.
* **Architecture:**
  * Automated scraping & NLP summarization pipelines built on Dialmate AI infrastructure.
  * Serverless image & scorecard card generators rendering dynamic assets in < 2.5 seconds.
  * WebSocket live push notification engine delivering scores faster than broadcast TV.
* **Speaker (Sayan):** *"While competitors spend ₹2.5L/month on development shops and expensive SaaS tooling, our technology is built 100% in-house. That unlocks an 86.4% gross margin from Day 1."*

### Slide 5: Hyper-Lean Creator Distribution Engine
* **Headline:** Bypassing Ad Agency Middlemen via 50+ Regional Micro-Creators.
* **Strategy:**
  * Contracting 35–50 tier-2/tier-3 cricket & football micro-influencers (10k–70k hyper-engaged followers) at ₹3,000–₹5,000/post.
  * Moderated WhatsApp VIP groups & Telegram channels where daily match score cards are seeded for viral 1-click forwarding.
  * Target blended Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) capped strictly at **< ₹4.00/user**.
* **Speaker (Rajrup):** *"We don't burn money on broad Meta ads. We sponsor regional creators who already have fan trust, delivering an immediate 4.2% install conversion rate."*
