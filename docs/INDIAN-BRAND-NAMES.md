# Indianised Brand Names For Investor Pitch

Working product concept: a cricket + football bulletin that feels as urgent as a score alert and as sharp as a newsroom front page.

Current working title in the repo: **MatchBrief**.

This document proposes India-first naming systems for pitch decks, domain checks, and trademark screening. None of these names are cleared legally — treat them as shortlists for counsel / registry search.

## Naming criteria (what investors respond to)

- Instant sport meaning in Hindi, Hinglish, or stadium slang
- Easy to say on a podcast and spell in a WhatsApp forward
- Works for both cricket and football (or clearly expandable)
- Feels premium enough for ads, not only meme-app energy
- Domain + Play Store / App Store availability likely contestable but not hopeless
- Avoids copying ESPN, Cricbuzz, Sportskeeda, RevSportz, OneFootball phonetics too closely

## Positioning frames you can attach to any shortlist name

1. **Bulletin, not dump** — one front page that decides what matters now.
2. **Live + lineage** — score state and editorial context in one habit loop.
3. **India dual-sport** — cricket and football as equal first-class products.
4. **Creator-native** — writers and presenters as followable objects, not sidebars.
5. **Honest free stack → paid depth** — free RSS/scores/embeds first; licensed live feeds later.

## Sample elevator pitch (swap in chosen name)

> **[Name]** is the sports bulletin for Indian fans who want the live pulse and the sharp takeaway on one screen. Cricket and football get equal priority. We start with a free, fast web experience — live modules, trusted briefs, and social signal — then expand into match centers, follows, and creator-led retention. The gap is not more content. The gap is clearer hierarchy and faster meaning.

## Domain and trademark hygiene checklist

Before locking a name in the deck:

- [ ] Exact `.in` and `.com` availability
- [ ] Google Play / App Store collision search
- [ ] Trademark search on Indian Class 41 / 42 adjacent marks
- [ ] Social handle availability on X, Instagram, YouTube
- [ ] Spoken test with 5 non-tech cricket fans and 5 football fans
- [ ] Check confusing similarity vs Cricbuzz, Crex, Sportstar, RevSportz, Sportskeeda
